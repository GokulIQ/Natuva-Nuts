The Natuva Advantage

Institutional Grade Supply with Farm Terroir Clarity

Traditional wholesale nut importers operate through multi-tiered broker chains that leave raw crops sitting in unconditioned maritime containers for months. Natuva contracts directly with certified grower cooperatives, ensuring cold-chain custody from the orchard gate to your loading dock.

Guaranteed Sizing & Count-Per-Pound

Strict W180/W240 cashew sizing and Nonpareil almond counts backed by batch certificates of analysis.

Zero Chemical Sulfur or Mineral Oil Glazing

Clean label ingredients suitable for certified organic, kosher, and halal food manufacturing.

Private Label & Co-Branded Packaging

Fulfillment Workflow

Seamless Commercial Onboarding

From initial laboratory sample dispatch to nationwide scheduled freight logistics.

01
Spec Consultation

Discuss your kernel sizing parameters, salt percentages, roast levels, and packaging specifications with an account director.

02
Sample Evaluation

We dispatch complimentary 500g testing kits along with formal laboratory certificates of analysis (COA) for your quality team.

03
Production Run

Small-batch copper hearth roasting and cleanroom nitrogen sealing executed under strict ISO and HACCP food hygiene protocols.

04
Cold-Chain Freight

Temperature-monitored refrigerated freight dispatched directly to your distribution center or individualized multi-address recipient lists.
