Sourcing & Quality Stories

Trace the journey of our dry fruits from sun-drenched family groves and high mountain terraces directly to our artisanal cleanroom.

Ethical Cultivation

Reverence for the Soil and the Hands That Till It

True connoisseurship begins long before roasting. It starts with soil vitality. Industrial monoculture forces fruit trees with synthetic nitrogen fertilizers that pump kernels full of water while diluting essential oils.

Natuva growers rely on restorative soil practices: clover cover crops that naturally fix nitrogen, native hedgerows that provide habitats for beneficial predatory insects, and micro-sprinklers that deliver water directly to the root zone without runoff.

Integrity Chain

The Cold-Chain Journey from Orchard to Pantry

How we protect fragile nut lipids against heat, light, and humidity at every transition point.

Stage 1
Dawn Tree Knocking

Nuts are harvested only when natural hull-split occurs, gathered during crisp morning hours before desert heat rises.

Stage 2
De-Hulling & Drying

Hulls are removed within 24 hours. Nuts are gently solar-cured or dried with warm air to exactly 5.5% moisture.

Stage 3
Climate Vault Transit

Stored in dark, humidity-controlled vaults at 10°C to prevent lipid peroxidation during intermediate transport.

Stage 4
Cleanroom Packing

Small-batch copper roasted, optical scanned, and flushed with 99.8% pure nitrogen into hermetic containers.

Technology in Service of Taste

Dual-Spectrum Optical Sorting

Even with hand-harvesting, microscopic defects can compromise an entire tin. A single insect-punctured cashew or bitter shriveled kernel ruins the culinary experience.

At our facility, harvest lots stream across dual-pass optical sorters equipped with high-speed pneumatic air jets. The sensors evaluate individual kernel geometry, density, and color 2,000 times per second, ejecting flawed nuts in milliseconds.

Only the top 3-5% of any harvest lot meets the visual and physical benchmarks required for Natuva packaging.
