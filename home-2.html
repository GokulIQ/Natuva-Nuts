Ethical Provenance

From Sunlit Orchard to Heirloom Box

Every Natuva harvest passes through an unhurried, multi-generational supply chain designed to honor the grower and protect the earth.

01
Microclimate Sourcing

Direct partnerships with multi-generational growers in California's Central Valley, Pahalgam Valley in Kashmir, and Malabar in Kerala.

02
Dual Optical Sorting

Advanced infrared and high-resolution optical cameras scan every single nut, filtering out microscopic surface fractures and uneven sizes.

03
Artisan Copper Roasting

Small-batch flame roasting in antique copper pans with unrefined Himalayan pink salt and cold-pressed organic coconut oil.

04
Inert Nitrogen Sealing

Hermetic sealing under 99.8% medical-grade nitrogen guarantees harvest-day crispness and aroma for up to 12 months.
