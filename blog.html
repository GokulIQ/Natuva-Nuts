10-Minute Recipe
Cardamom-Spiced Medjool Energy Truffles

Blend 10 pitted Royal Medjool dates with 1 cup of raw Kashmiri walnut halves, 1 tbsp raw cacao, and a pinch of ground green cardamom. Roll into truffles and dust with emerald pistachio powder.

Prep Time: 10 mins · Yields: 14 truffles · 0g Refined Sugar
Overnight Breakfast
Ancient Grain Soaked Almond Bircher

Soak rolled oats, chia seeds, and 12 slivered Mammoth California almonds overnight in oat milk with grated organic green apple and a spoon of raw acacia honey. Serve chilled.

Prep Time: 5 mins + chill · Servings: 2 · High Fiber & Vitamin E
