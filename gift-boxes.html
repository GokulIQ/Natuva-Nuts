Heirloom Standard

Craftsmanship That Endures Long After the Feast

Unlike disposable cardboard gift packaging, Natuva hampers are heirloom objects designed for permanent display.

Pure Handcrafted Woods & Brass

Crafted from sustainable Sheesham and aromatic cedarwood with antiqued brass catches and hand-hammered serving canisters.

Individual Hermetic Canisters

Each nut variety resides in its own nitrogen-flushed, induction-sealed tin, ensuring that opening one jar does not compromise the freshness of others.

Sustainable & Plastic-Free

Zero single-use plastics. Packaging materials are 100% biodegradable or permanently reusable as luxury tableware and jewelry storage.
